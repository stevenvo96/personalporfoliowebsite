About Me

Hello! I’m a registered nurse transitioning into a cloud career. I bring three years of experience working in high-pressure acute care settings, where I developed a strong foundation in problem-solving, critical thinking, and customer support. I’m currently enrolled in the AWS Cloud Institute, where I’m gaining hands-on experience with core AWS services, cloud architecture, and troubleshooting

My goal is to become a highly skilled cloud engineer who designs, supports, and optimizes secure cloud solutions. I also aim to grow into a leadership role, helping organizations innovate through cloud technologies and mentoring others entering the field.
